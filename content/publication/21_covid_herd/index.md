abstract: |
  We assess the feasibility of reaching the herd immunity threshold against SARS-CoV-2 through
  vaccination, considering vaccine effectiveness (VE), transmissibility of the virus and the level of
  pre-existing immunity in populations, as well as their age structure. If highly transmissible
  variants of concern become dominant in areas with low levels of naturally-acquired immunity and/or
  in populations with large proportions of < 15 year-olds, control of infection without
  non-pharmaceutical interventions may only be possible with a VE ≥ 80%, and coverage extended to
  children.
